## Product Overview

Eside is an anonymous experience-sharing platform where people can document real-life experiences, incidents, struggles, failures, decisions, and outcomes, allowing others to learn from collective human experience rather than isolated opinions.

Unlike traditional social platforms where stories disappear into endless feeds, Eside structures experiences into searchable knowledge that helps people understand:

- What happened
    
- What actions people took
    
- What worked
    
- What failed
    
- What happened afterward
    

The goal is to build a living repository of human experiences and outcomes.

---

# Problem Statement

People face challenges every day but often struggle to find people who truly understand their situation.

Current platforms have major limitations:

- Experiences are scattered across multiple platforms.
    
- Valuable stories become difficult to find later.
    
- Discussions focus on opinions rather than outcomes.
    
- Users rarely see long-term results from decisions.
    
- Similar experiences cannot be easily compared.
    

As a result, people repeatedly ask questions that thousands of others have already faced.

Examples:

- How did people recover after failing a semester?
    
- What happened after changing careers?
    
- How did people overcome loneliness?
    
- What helped after a difficult breakup?
    
- What actions improved financial situations?
    

There is no structured platform dedicated to collecting and organizing lived experiences and their outcomes.

# MVP Features

## Anonymous Accounts

Users create anonymous profiles.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Username required
    
- Email verification required
    
- Real name optional
    
- Anonymous identity preserved
    

---

## Experience Creation

Users submit experiences.

Fields:

- Title
    
- Story
    
- Category
    
- Tags
    

Acceptance Criteria:

- Experience stored successfully
    
- Appears in community feed
    
- Linked to anonymous profile

# Risks

## Content Moderation

Users may post harmful or inappropriate content.

Mitigation:

- Reporting tools
    
- Community guidelines
    
- Moderation workflows
    

---

## Empty Feed Problem

Users may leave if there is insufficient content.

Mitigation:

- Seed experiences
    
- Invite early contributors
    
- Focused launch community
    

---

## Low Follow-Up Rates

Users may not return to update outcomes.

Mitigation:

- Reminder notifications
    
- Simple update workflow
