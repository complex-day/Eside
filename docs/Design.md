## Product Overview

Eside is an anonymous experience-sharing platform that helps people learn from real-life experiences, incidents, failures, struggles, decisions, and outcomes.

Unlike traditional social platforms where stories disappear into feeds, Eside structures experiences into searchable knowledge and community-driven insights.

The platform combines:

- Anonymous storytelling
    
- Community discussions
    
- Outcome tracking
    
- Experience analytics
    

---

# Design Vision

### Core Feeling

Users should feel:

> "I am not alone."

and

> "I can learn from people who have already gone through this."

---

# Design Principles

## 1. Human First

Stories are more important than statistics.

Users should connect with experiences before viewing analytics.

---

## 2. Safe Anonymity

Users should feel comfortable sharing sensitive experiences without revealing personal identity.

---

## 3. Insight Driven

Every experience should contribute to collective knowledge.

---

## 4. Low Friction

Reading and sharing should require minimal effort.

---

## 5. Mobile First

The platform is designed primarily for mobile users and adapted for desktop.

1. Discover people who experienced similar situations.
    
2. Learn from real outcomes.
    
3. Contribute their own experiences safely.
    
4. Transform individual stories into collective human knowledge.
